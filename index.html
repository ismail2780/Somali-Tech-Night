About the event

A night built for the Somali tech community

Somali Tech Night brings together developers, designers, founders and curious students for an evening of talks, ideas and good conversation in the heart of Oslo.

Whether you are writing your first line of code or leading a team, you will leave with new knowledge, new friends and fresh inspiration to build what comes next.

  • 💡

    Learn

    Practical talks from people working in the industry.

  • 🤝

    Connect

    Meet peers, mentors and future collaborators.

    Explore

    Discover new tools, careers and opportunities.
